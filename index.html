How it works

The paper trail
An event leaves a record before it happens. A mall cannot open without an occupancy certificate. A listed retailer must tell the stock exchange. A brand posts “opening soon” on its own grid first.
Signals, ranked by lead time
Fifty signals across ten channels — police permissions, civic records, licensing, exchange filings, venue calendars, trade press, social. Each carries its tested access status.
Scored, not guessed
Every opportunity is ranked on priority, stage, sector and whether it can actually be reached — so the best call is always first.
Rescanned on a schedule
The pipeline is rebuilt from source automatically, and the build verifies itself before publishing. The timestamp at the top is that scan.
